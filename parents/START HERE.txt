MEDIA & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
Parent Coffee · 18 November 2026 · HS504

Double-click “Start Presentation.command” to open the local browser deck.
Keep this folder and its contents together.

F              Full screen
Right / Left   Next animation / previous freeze frame
K              Pause or resume
R              Replay the current scene
G              Jump to any slide
P              Presenter notes and audience window
?              Help

Every scene plays its animation once, then holds still for you to talk over.
There are 31 click-steps. Nothing advances on its own.
The deck works locally with Wi-Fi turned off.
