Flint K12

Flint is an AI platform built for schools. This optional guide explores turning your own teaching materials into an interactive or a short explainer video. You can return to it after the main workshop.

Best for

Guardrails and student-facing safety. Your materials are the boundary: Flint builds from what you give it.

Exercise 2: Make an interactive

Everything starts in the box on Flint’s Home page. You describe what you want, add your materials, and choose what kind of thing Flint should make.

  1. Type your prompt in How can I help you today?
  2. Add your materials with the paperclip: slides, readings or notes.
  3. Press the magic wand and choose Live simulation for an interactive, or Video for an explainer. Then send.
