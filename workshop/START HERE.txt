Double-click “Start Presentation.command” to open the local browser deck.
Keep this folder and its contents together.

F              Full screen
Right / Left   Next animation / previous freeze frame
K              Pause or resume
R              Replay the current scene
G              Jump to any slide
P              Presenter notes and audience window
?              Help

Move your mouse to reveal the controls. Timers hold at zero until you advance.
The complete deck works locally with Wi-Fi turned off.

README.md includes GitHub Pages upload instructions.
TEST-RESULTS.md records the checks and browser test limits.
