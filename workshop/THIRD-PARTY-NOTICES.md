# Bundled presentation dependencies

The package includes the unmodified HyperFrames player and slideshow bundles from
the project's pinned **HyperFrames 0.8.81** installation. HyperFrames declares the
Apache License 2.0; see `vendor/HyperFrames-LICENSE.txt` and
https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes .

**GSAP 3.14.2** is bundled as `vendor/gsap.min.js`. Its original copyright and
license notice remain at the beginning of that file. Copyright 2025 GreenSock.
The original notice points to https://gsap.com/standard-license .

The bundled **Montserrat** font is provided under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
see `vendor/Montserrat-OFL.txt`.

The artwork, screenshots, recorded illustrations and supplied music come from the
existing workshop project. This export does not grant a new license to those assets.
